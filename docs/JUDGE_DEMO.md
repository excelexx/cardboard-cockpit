# Continuous judge demo

The demo has one flow: **Play → calibration → takeoff → fly and shoot → land when ready**; **Set Up Cardboard** retains the full control tutorial. Former Tutorial entry points are compatibility aliases for this same experience.

## Setup cameras

The laptop and phone panels are equally sized at 724 × 480 layout pixels. Each complete camera image fits inside its panel without cropping or distortion. A 16:9 image displays at approximately 712 × 400 pixels, about 2.8 times the previous visible image area. Instructions, live control values, connection status and setup buttons remain below the previews.

## Takeoff and flight

Increase throttle and pull up as speed builds. After takeoff, press **badge A / G** to retract gear and flaps together. Keyboard A performs the same action; F changes flaps alone. Comma and period control keyboard rudder.

Show plasma gun tag ID 4 or hold Space/left mouse for two adaptive beams. Cover the tag or release the input to stop. During large flocks, two automatic missiles can launch together every five seconds when suitable targets are available. The electronic badge handles secondary controls, not weapons or steering.

Waves spawn ahead of the current heading and pitch, in loose packets of 1–4 birds with a fresh random 200–500 metre gap between groups. If you leave a flock behind, a fresh encounter follows after a short break; missed birds never count as kills. New waves keep arriving: 6, then 8, then 10, and then 12 geese per wave. The HUD shows **WAVE N**, actual kills in the current wave and elapsed flight time. There is no countdown, fixed number of waves or landing kill quota.

## Landing on your terms

Press **B / badge B** . Landing mode starts a little farther out (3.4 km), with a reduce-speed prompt, while leaving steering, pitch and throttle under your control. Press B again to reset the approach to that starting position. Press **badge A / G** to put gear and flaps down, reduce power, and fly your approach. B remains the landing control; the central coaching card and landing button have been removed.

Touchdown on dry airport ground stays planted while you slow down. Water or off-airport contact instead starts an automatic return to the runway. The demo does not demand a scored precision landing or make you clear a fixed target quota before you can finish.

## Readability and comfort

The in-flight overlay uses a central symmetric attitude ladder, floating compass and transparent readouts. Guide rings and duplicate lesson cards do not compete with it. Sound starts enabled and can be muted with M. Only confirmed kills add camera shake, at 40% below the original amplitude. Firing, boost, terrain proximity and touchdown do not shake the camera.

The flight overlay has a heading compass at the top, a pitch/bank ladder and altitude around the crosshair, and only speed, power, pitch, yaw and bank in the lower-left. Larger camera previews sit in the lower-right flush with the bottom and right edges, without tracking footers or surrounding panels. The jet starts stationary at zero throttle; lost-yoke recovery pauses sounds without replaying loops or chimes.
