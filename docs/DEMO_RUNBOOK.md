# Judge demo — 0.18.0

1. Open **Cardboard Cockpit.app**. Its helper connects the powered electronic badge. For cardboard controls, start **Launch Two-Camera Cockpit.command** with the phone connected.
2. Choose **Play**. The former Tutorial entry point opens this same continuous demo. **Use keyboard** in setup bypasses cameras; **Watch demo** flies and fires automatically.
3. Both large camera previews show the full image without cropping. Hold the yoke upright for neutral capture, test throttle idle/full/idle, then show/cover/show plasma gun tag ID 4. Centre the yoke, idle the throttle and cover the tag to start.
4. Increase throttle and pull up as speed builds. Once airborne, press **badge A / keyboard G** to retract gear and flaps. Keyboard A is also a gear/flap shortcut; comma and period provide rudder steering.
5. Show ID 4 or hold Space/left mouse to fire both adaptive plasma beams. Cover or release to stop. Large flocks receive automatic two-missile bursts at up to one burst every five seconds, when eligible targets are available.
6. Keep flying and shooting as new waves arrive. The HUD shows elapsed time, **WAVE N** and the current wave's real kill count. There is no fixed quota or countdown.
7. Press **B / badge B**, or **LAND NOW**, whenever you want to land. This starts landing mode; you still control the approach. Press **badge A / G** for gear and flaps down, reduce throttle, and steer and pitch toward the runway. Live coach text shows gear, flap and power state. Touchdown stays planted while the aircraft slows.
8. HOME/Escape pauses. LEFT/V changes view; RIGHT or held DOWN shows the badge tactical view. R restarts the demo. M mutes the session; sound starts enabled at launch.

If the yoke disappears, flight pauses. Bring it back and hold steady, or choose **Use keyboard**. Throttle changes blend smoothly after reacquisition. Settings retains independent sensitivity and agility for all three axes plus aiming assistance.

Use one Bluetooth relay for the badge. Automated checks use synthetic camera pictures and badge packets; they do not verify physical placement or live hardware. The larger preview layout preserves the two cameras independently.
