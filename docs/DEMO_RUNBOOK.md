# Short guided flight demo

**Play / Check Controls** runs a three-second yoke calibration, throttle checks, a four-second yoke overview (no movement checks), and the gun grip/cover/uncover checks, then puts the aircraft at rest on the departure runway. Advance the throttle and lift off through the gold rings. Cyan rings show the next few gates. The on-screen arrow points back to a missed or off-screen ring.

After the three departure rings, the aircraft reaches cruising altitude and **sixteen goose targets** are distributed along 7.5 km of the route, 500 metres apart with varied heights and left/right offsets. Uncover the gun tag to fire. Targets stay available until shot; there are no replacement waves or time-based skips. The counter shows progress out of sixteen.

Shoot the geese while following the high cruise rings. The destination starts roughly **27 km ahead**, offset slightly to the right. San Francisco cruise rings reach about 940 metres (3,100 feet) above sea level. Landing preparation begins only after all sixteen geese are cleared and the cruise gates are completed; slower shooters get an extended cruise ahead. As you enter the descent, prompts react to the aircraft: reduce throttle, lower gear, deploy landing flaps, then follow the descending rings. Missing a ring cannot block landing: the forward runway corridor permits descent even before gear is deployed, and the actual runway surface controls touchdown. Terrain protection remains active elsewhere. Pulling the cardboard throttle below 15% also activates airbraking while above 95 m/s on return. After touchdown the demo brakes automatically, and the result appears once the aircraft stops. Landing before clearing all sixteen does not complete the demo.

**Watch demo** flies and shoots the same route automatically. The guided run takes about **five minutes**, excluding the camera tutorial; manual runs depend on the pilot. Gear/flap prompts read the existing aircraft state; the separate badge integration can drive that state without changes to mission progression. Normal Play keeps the yoke, throttle and gun under the player's control. **H** toggles flight assistance when using keyboard controls. **Esc** pauses; Settings retains independent sensitivity, agility and auto-aim controls.

San Francisco remains the default map. The destination runway is built ahead of the cruising route with a small lateral offset; it no longer follows the long city sightseeing route. Landing practice remains a separate airborne exercise.

Validation: `test_demo_mission.gd` exercises a complete continuous takeoff, sixteen real cannon kills during the high cruise, ring-guided gentle approach and landing, including the real San Francisco height grid when launched with `-- --sf`. No camera frames or user settings are written by the test.
