# Runway music

`runway_music.mp3` is a 15-minute cut of the user-provided `Free Bird Solo 1 Hour.mp3`.

- Source interval: 00:12–15:12.
- The first five seconds fade in, corresponding to source 00:12–00:17.
- Encoded as stereo 44.1 kHz MP3 at 128 kbit/s to reduce bundle size.
- Each runway start resets playback. Calibration/title screens do not play it. Tracking holds pause it, and playback continues through touchdown, rollout and results until the main menu. It loops if a flight lasts longer than 15 minutes.

The original downloaded MP3 is unchanged.
