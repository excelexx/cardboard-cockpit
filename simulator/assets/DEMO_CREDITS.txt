# Demo assets and research

- **Title key art:** generated with the built-in image generation tool and stored at `simulator/assets/art/goose-title.png`. The available tool does not expose a selectable “2.5” model version. Prompt: cinematic 16:9 premium stylized 3D game background; giant recognizable Canada goose, training jet and saucer-shaped freighter over a sunset Canadian campus/alpine valley; subjects on the right, dark negative space on the left; detailed feathers/metal; no text, logos or HUD.
- **Canada goose:** “Goose” by Poly by Google, [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza/m/9wn3If7Qgb4), CC BY 3.0. Original model is included; scaled/reoriented with animated wing attachments. License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/.
- **Small and advanced ships / freighter detailing:** [Kenney Space Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/space-kit), CC0. Original GLBs and license included. The Falcon-style saucer and front mandibles are original fan-made geometry with Kenney detailing, not an official licensed Star Wars model.
- **Weapon and impact effects:** [Kenney Sci-fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds), CC0. Selected OGG files and license included.
- **Engine/wind and cannon recordings:** FlightGear AN-225 by Herbert Wagner (GPL-2.0) and F-35B (GPL-3.0). Original samples, notices, pinned download URLs and SHA-256 hashes are included in aircraft source manifests. No war-footage audio was scraped.
- **Music:** “Space Adventure” by MintoDog, [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/space-adventure), CC0; original OGG.
- **Goose calls:** Canada Geese, recorded by Lawrence Shove in 1966; copyright The British Library Board; [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Canada_Geese_(Branta_canadensis)_(W1CDR0001421_BD11).ogg), CC BY-SA 4.0. Original unmodified OGG included. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.
- **An-225 model:** Herbert Wagner, liveries by eagle, [source repository](https://github.com/HerbyW/AN-225-Mrija), GPL-2.0. Source model, texture, notices and reproducible conversion retained. The hangar and flight use a shared meter scale normalized to the catalog span/length. The [manufacturer](https://www.antonov.com/en/history/an-225-mriya) lists 88.4 m span and 84 m length.

Public-only background reading: [Navy directed-energy training](https://www.navsea.navy.mil/Media/News/Article/4494885/navys-directed-energy-systems-integration-lab-to-train-sailors-on-laser-weapons/), [Marine Corps counter-UAS overview](https://www.pae.marines.mil/Industry-Portal/Counter-UAS-Counter-UxS/), and [CIA's public OXCART history](https://www.cia.gov/legacy/headquarters/a-12-oxcart/). These informed broad presentation ideas only; gameplay uses invented tuning and does not reproduce operational weapon systems. No classified material was accessed.

## Radio, landing and weapon-design update

The voice lines are **licensed recordings**, not ElevenLabs generations: [Kenney Voiceover Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/voiceover-pack), CC0, male voice Jeffrey M. Smith and female voice Giselle. Twenty short event cues are assembled from the original recordings with narrow-band EQ, compression, light static and radio squelch. Sources, credits, processed WAVs and SHA-256 manifest are shipped under `assets/audio/radio`. `tools/build_radio_assets.py` reproduces the processing with ffmpeg and NumPy; the app itself needs neither tool nor a network connection. ElevenLabs was not configured; licensed clips were selected for this revision.

Gear, flaps, wheel rolling and touchdown sounds use the same pinned GPL-2.0 FlightGear An-225 source library. Kenney Sci-fi Sounds supplies cockpit electronics and the beam hum. Music stays at its selected volume during speech; engine/effect channels duck during radio speech; pause preserves playback position and mute silences all layers.

Detailed missile models: Jarlan Perez and Poly by Google, [seeker](https://poly.pizza/m/1Xid2Qhqn2s) and [battery missile](https://poly.pizza/m/dPVCvXP-S58), CC BY 3.0. Smoke and impact sprites: [Kenney Particle Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack), CC0. Plasma-emitter chassis: [Kenney Blaster Kit 2.1](https://kenney.nl/assets/blaster-kit), CC0, with added magnetic rings, iris, materials and ship mounts.

`assets/art/plasma-cannon.png` was made using the built-in image generator. Final prompt: a premium ship-mounted plasma-cannon illustration, three-quarter view, dark titanium chassis, off-white ceramic armor, copper cooling lines, three cyan electromagnetic rings, hexagonal muzzle iris, restrained amber hazard accents and no text. Runtime emitters are modeled adaptations of the licensed Kenney chassis; the illustration is not a generated 3D mesh.
