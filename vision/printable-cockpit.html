CARDBOARD COCKPIT

Two paper markers turn ordinary cardboard into flight controls. Print at 100% / actual size, with browser headers and footers disabled.

The white border inside the dashed line is part of each marker: cut on the line, not around the black square. Mount flat, facing the webcam; keep fingers and shiny tape away from the pattern. Do not show spare copies to the camera.

01 / BUILD THE YOKE

Laminate two 32 × 20 cm cardboard U shapes; remove a centered 16 × 12 cm rectangle from the top. Add folded ribs and taped handles.

Mount ID 7 on an 11 × 11 cm center plate; lean it back 10–15° if the webcam sits low. Rotate for roll; tilt the top forward/backward for pitch. Rest your wrists; keep the marker visible.

02 / BUILD THE THROTTLE

Cut a 20 × 1.2 cm slot in a shoebox lid. Fit a 10 × 8 cm sliding carriage between two underside guide rails. Tape a capped pen upright as the grip.

Mount ID 23 on an 8 × 8 cm upright plate that moves with the carriage. Label nearest end IDLE, middle CRUISE, far end FULL.

03 / SET UP & CALIBRATE

Webcam: 70–100 cm in front, 25–40 cm above desk, aimed down. Keep both markers in view. Raise or offset it if throttle travel is hard to see. Run the tracker with --camera 0 --check, sweep both controls, press Q and fix any FAIL line. Then run --camera 0 --calibrate: hold each pose for one second and press Space: neutral → left → right → forward → back → idle → full. Center the yoke, idle the throttle, select vision in the game, and start.
