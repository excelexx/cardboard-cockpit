CARDBOARD COCKPIT

A yoke tag and three relative throttle tags turn cardboard into flight controls. Print at 100% / actual size, with browser headers and footers disabled.

Keep the entire white margin around each marker. Mount flat, facing the webcam; keep fingers and shiny tape away from the pattern. Do not show spare copies to the camera.

01 / BUILD THE YOKE

Laminate two 32 × 20 cm cardboard U shapes; remove a centered 16 × 12 cm rectangle from the top. Add folded ribs and taped handles.

Mount ID 7 on an 11 × 11 cm center plate. Rotate for roll; tilt the top forward/backward for pitch. Rest your wrists; keep the marker visible.

02 / BUILD THE THROTTLE

Build a slider with about 10 cm travel. Put fixed tags 0 and 2 at its two ends. Tag 1 moves beside them on a parallel track; at each stop its center lines up across the track with the endpoint.

Keep all three faces in the same flat plane. Leave the fixed tags visible throughout travel. The endpoint tags define the range live.

03 / SET UP & CALIBRATE

Aim the camera down toward all markers. Start tools/tracker.sh --camera 0 --paper-test and hold yoke 7 still to center. Or use --throttle-only with keyboard steering. Launch the game with tools/run.sh -- --stickers. Throttle needs no endpoint calibration. Keep spare tags out of view.
